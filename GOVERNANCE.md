# Governance

## Maintainers

Maintainers review and merge changes, decide on the direction of the
project within the [vision and scope](VISION.md), and are responsible for
releases.

## Decisions

Most decisions are made in issues and pull requests by lazy consensus: a
proposal is accepted if no maintainer objects within a reasonable time. If
maintainers disagree, they discuss it in the open and decide by majority of
maintainers.

Changes to the vision and scope or to this document need the approval of a
majority of maintainers.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have made sustained contributions, such as code, reviews or
upstream fixes, can be nominated by any maintainer. A nomination is accepted
with the approval of a majority of maintainers.

Maintainers who are no longer active may step down or be moved to an
emeritus status.

## Changes as the project grows

While the project has a single maintainer, decisions rest with that
maintainer. This document will be revised as more maintainers join.
